Who We Are

Atagul Games is an independent game and technology studio focused on building interactive experiences, experimental games, and creative software tools. We operate with an indie mindset while aiming for professional quality and long-term growth.

Our studio combines gameplay design, technical research, and rapid prototyping. We value learning, iteration, and originality over shortcuts or trends.
