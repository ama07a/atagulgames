Who We Are

Atagul Games is an independent game and technology studio founded in Türkiye. The studio focuses on developing original games, experimental interactive projects, and software tools that support creative development.

We operate with an indie mindset while aiming for professional production standards. Our work combines gameplay design, technical experimentation, and research-driven development. Rather than following trends blindly, we prioritize learning, iteration, and originality.

Atagul Games is not only a game studio but also a creative laboratory where ideas are tested, refined, and transformed into playable experiences.

Our Mission

Our mission is to create meaningful interactive experiences by blending creativity, technology, and experimentation.

We aim to:

  • Develop original and accessible games
  • Experiment with new mechanics and systems
  • Build tools that help developers learn and create
  • Continuously improve through research and iteration

Atagul Labs (R&D)

Atagul Labs is our internal research and development space. This is where ideas are tested, systems are prototyped, and experimental concepts are explored.

The lab focuses on:

  • Gameplay experimentation
  • Tool and engine research
  • Automation and productivity tools
  • Educational and learning-oriented projects

Not every experiment becomes a public product. However, each experiment contributes to our technical knowledge and creative growth.

Tools & Technology

ATAGUL IDE

ATAGUL IDE is a lightweight development environment created to support learning, experimentation, and small-scale projects.

The tool focuses on simplicity, speed, and accessibility, making it suitable for beginners as well as indie developers who prefer minimal workflows.
